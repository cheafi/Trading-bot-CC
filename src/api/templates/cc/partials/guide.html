📘 Random Walk — Global Principles (8) Expand
Constitution layer — apply on every surface (Dashboard, Playbook, Dossier, Portfolio, Funds). Read net edge after cost; humility labels are supportive, not excuses to trade.
1. Markets are noisy in the short run — favor regime and process over daily noise.
2. Page-level regime overrides single-name temptation — board gate beats a pretty card.
3. Research relevance does not equal deploy permission — Discovery/Flow/Funds/RS inform only.
4. Cost-adjusted edge matters more than raw signal strength — read Raw · Net after cost.
5. Portfolio construction matters more than isolated ideas — correlated stories are one bet.
6. Cash is a valid allocation — patience when edge is unclear is risk management.
7. Avoid overconfidence in forecasting — scenarios and invalidation beat point predictions.
8. Bubble conditions require extra skepticism — crowding and extension need explicit discount.
Humility labels: supportive only · likely priced in · evidence incomplete · monitoring only · not enough advantage over passive baseline · research signal only · narrative-rich, evidence-light · cost drag may erase weak edge · high structure, low predictive certainty

Buffett principles (owner judgment): understand the business · economic quality and moat · honest capital allocation · stay inside circle of competence · patience when board says WAIT — see docs/BUFFETT_BIOGRAPHY_UPGRADE.md

📖 Layer 2 — How to Read the Platform Expand
🔝 Header bar — 4 layers
Status is four layers, not just LIVE/PAPER/SYNTHETIC. Check all four before trusting any card:
① Scoped truth strip (header example)
Educational example — live header uses Market:/Board:/Brief:/Broker:/Runtime:/Authority: labels.
Market: Fresh · Board: Stale · Brief: Expired 21d · Broker: Offline · Runtime: Stale · Authority: Blocked
REAL_TIME SYNTHETIC
⚠ Any scope Stale / Expired / Fallback / SYNTHETIC — treat affected prices and scores as provisional until that scope recovers and Authority: Open.
② Engine status
LIVE PAPER OFF BREAKER
Confirm in header and Ops when freshness matters. LIVE/PAPER badge alone is not always proof a fresh runtime cycle completed. OFF = stale decisions. BREAKER blocks new entries.
③ Broker status
READY PAPER DISCONNECTED
Gateway may show LOGIN or GATEWAY UP · LOGIN REQUIRED — transport up, session not active. Execution-ready requires READY + account + bracket alignment, not Connect alone.
④ Execution readiness
STRONG_TRADE TRADE SELECTIVE WAIT NO_TRADE
Dashboard tradeability banner + per-card execution_ready flag. WAIT/NO_TRADE: board-level hold.
Trust strip (below nav): regime (BULL/SIDEWAYS/CHOPPY/BEAR), VIX, breadth, and confidence bar. In BEAR or CHOPPY, board gates often downgrade cards to WAIT/AVOID even when individual scores look fine.

📚 Layer 3 — Reference Manual Expand
🗂 Three surface types
Deploy surfaces Core
Dashboard · Playbook · Portfolio & Risk — deploy/no-deploy and sizing when board gate + authority align.
Confirm surfaces CONFIRM ONLY
Search / Dossier — structure confirmation only; not a deploy verdict; no standalone deploy permission.
Research / supporting Research-only
Discovery · Funds · Flow · RS — idea generation and context; not trade permission alone.
Diagnostic / execution Diagnostic Execution-dependent
IBKR · Ops · Rejections · Guide — handoff, health/jobs, blocker audit. Ops confirms runtime; IBKR required for live sends.
🛑 When NOT to act — full list
Engine OFFMode pill grey or Ops shows no fresh cycle — decisions stale or absent.
Board gate closedWAIT/NO_TRADE tradeability — board-level hold.
Broker not readyLOGIN-only (GATEWAY UP · LOGIN REQUIRED), disconnected, or portfolio/bracket misaligned.
Bracket not readyCard lacks entry/stop or execution_ready flag.
Mock flowFlow surface in synthetic/mock mode — colour only.
Backtest fundsFunds sleeves marked research/backtest — not live capital.
Dossier confirm-onlyConfirm-only · 僅結構確認 — 價位僅供參考，live 載入前不可定倉或交接 IBKR。
No live validationAny scoped strip shows Stale/Expired/Fallback, Broker: Offline, Runtime stale, Authority: Blocked, or SYNTHETIC mode.
Scanner warmingDiscovery loading or 0 hits on WAIT day — often correct, not a miss.
Manual sync pendingPortfolio or broker positions not reconciled after last session.
💬 Non-decision triggers (context only)
These inform judgment but do not justify an entry alone:
• AI commentary — Dashboard narrative and Dossier "Why Now"
• Mock / synthetic flow — options prints without live validation
• Insider / 13F filings — lagged supporting intel in Dossier
• Funds / Backtest Lab — backtest research only; not deployment authority
• RS leaderboard alone — must align with Playbook action, regime, and board gate
