📘 隨機漫步原則 (8) · Random Walk — Global Principles 展開 · Expand
Constitution layer — apply on every surface (Dashboard, Playbook, Dossier, Portfolio, Funds). Read net edge after cost; humility labels are supportive, not excuses to trade.
1. Markets are noisy in the short run — favor regime and process over daily noise.
2. Page-level regime overrides single-name temptation — board gate beats a pretty card.
3. Research relevance does not equal deploy permission — Discovery/Flow/Funds/RS inform only.
4. Cost-adjusted edge matters more than raw signal strength — read Raw · Net after cost.
5. Portfolio construction matters more than isolated ideas — correlated stories are one bet.
6. Cash is a valid allocation — patience when edge is unclear is risk management.
7. Avoid overconfidence in forecasting — scenarios and invalidation beat point predictions.
8. Bubble conditions require extra skepticism — crowding and extension need explicit discount.

📖 第 2 層 — 如何解讀平台 · Layer 2 — How to Read the Platform 展開 · Expand
🔝 Header bar — 4 layers
Status is four layers, not just LIVE/PAPER/SYNTHETIC. Check all four before trusting any card:
① Market data status
REAL_TIME DATA FRESH STALE SYNTHETIC
⚠ STALE / CRITICAL / SYNTHETIC — treat all prices and scores as provisional until DATA FRESH returns.
② Engine status
LIVE PAPER OFF BREAKER
Confirm in header and Ops when freshness matters. LIVE/PAPER badge alone is not always proof a fresh runtime cycle completed. OFF = stale decisions. BREAKER blocks new entries.
③ Broker status
READY PAPER DISCONNECTED
Gateway may show LOGIN or GATEWAY UP · LOGIN REQUIRED — transport up, session not active. Execution-ready requires READY + account + bracket alignment, not Connect alone.
④ Execution readiness
STRONG_TRADE TRADE SELECTIVE WAIT NO_TRADE
Dashboard tradeability banner + per-card execution_ready flag. WAIT/NO_TRADE days: patience is the active decision.
Trust strip (below nav): regime (BULL/SIDEWAYS/CHOPPY/BEAR), VIX, breadth, and confidence bar. In BEAR or CHOPPY, board gates often downgrade cards to WAIT/AVOID even when individual scores look fine.
🗓 Core workflow (current nav names)
1
🎯 Dashboard Core START HERE
Morning posture: regime, VIX, breadth, tradeability (WAIT/TRADE/NO_TRADE), best TRADE/WATCH/PILOT, execution readiness, and AI narrative. Legacy name: Overview. Pre-market brief content may appear here — there is no separate Brief tab in primary nav.
2
📋 Playbook Core
Ranked signal book with TRADE / PILOT / WATCH / AVOID filters. Legacy name: Ranked. Each row shows action badge, score, entry/stop/target, R:R, and execution_ready when bracket math is complete. As a default operating rule, prefer TRADE + execution_ready for full size; use PILOT for half-size probes; treat WATCH as monitor-only unless Dossier upgrades the verdict.
3
🔍 Search / Dossier Core
Per-ticker decision card — confirm before entry. Check in order:
Entry ZoneExpected clean entry range. Wider zone often means BUY_ON_DIP or PILOT sizing. Hard StopStructural exit level. Confirm-only dossier shows No sizing guidance in confirm-only mode — no IBKR handoff until live fetch succeeds. Target 1 / 2Reward anchors at ~2R and ~3R — guidance, not guaranteed fills. Factor ChipsGreen = tailwind, red = headwind. Weigh against regime and Playbook action. Sector PeersPeer confirmation adds context; absence is not auto-reject. Why NowAI thesis — supporting narrative, not a standalone trigger.
4
💼 Portfolio & Risk Core
Capacity check before adding risk: open positions, sector exposure, correlation, heat gauge, drawdown alerts. Legacy name: Portfolio. REBALANCE suggestions here are advisory — confirm against Playbook and broker state.
5
🔬 Discovery Research-only
Scanner hub for Leaders / Breakouts / Pullbacks / Flow hits. Legacy name: Scanners. Useful for idea generation; may return 0 hits on WAIT/NO_TRADE days or while warming. Cross-check any name in Playbook before acting.

💬 Non-decision triggers (context only)
These inform judgment but do not justify an entry alone:
• AI commentary — Dashboard narrative and Dossier "Why Now"
• Mock / synthetic flow — options prints without live validation
• Insider / 13F filings — lagged supporting intel in Dossier
• Funds / Backtest Lab — backtest research only; not deployment authority
• RS leaderboard alone — must align with Playbook action, regime, and board gate
